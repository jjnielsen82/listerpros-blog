Determining the Right Number of Photos for Your Property

There's no one-size-fits-all answer, but assessing your property's specifics will guide you. Start by considering size, features, and buyer demographics in your Arizona market.

Property Size Guidelines

  • Smaller Homes or Condos (under 1,500 sq ft): Aim for 20-30 photos. Cover essentials like the kitchen, living areas, and bedrooms, plus a few exteriors to show community amenities in places like Tempe.
  • Mid-Sized Homes (1,500-3,000 sq ft): 30-40 photos work best. Include detailed shots of key rooms and outdoor spaces, ideal for Phoenix ranch-style homes.
  • Larger Estates (over 4,000 sq ft): Go for 40-50 photos to showcase expansive layouts, such as those in upscale Scottsdale neighborhoods.
