Avoiding Common Mistakes

To get the most from 3D tours, steer clear of pitfalls that can undermine your efforts.

  1. Poor Quality Scans: Don't settle for low-res or dimly lit tours; they misrepresent spacious Arizona homes. Hire pros to capture details like high ceilings accurately.
  2. Overloading Features: Keep annotations minimal to avoid overwhelming users, especially on mobile—crucial in 2026.
  3. Neglecting Mobile: Test on smartphones to prevent high abandonment rates among commuting buyers.
  4. Ignoring Accessibility: Add voice-guided options for inclusivity, broadening appeal in markets like Tucson.
  5. Not Updating: Refresh tours for seasonal changes, like monsoons, to keep content current.
