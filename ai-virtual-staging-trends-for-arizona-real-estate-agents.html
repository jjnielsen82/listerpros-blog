Avoiding Common Pitfalls

While AI is powerful, missteps can hinder results. Over-relying on automation without reviews often leads to unrealistic scales—always check for Arizona specifics like low ceilings in older homes. Ignoring local aesthetics, such as skipping Southwestern styles, reduces appeal. Failing to disclose virtual elements breaches transparency guidelines, eroding trust. Neglect mobile optimization, and stagings may distort on devices. Lastly, overloading with options causes decision fatigue—limit to 2-3 per property.
