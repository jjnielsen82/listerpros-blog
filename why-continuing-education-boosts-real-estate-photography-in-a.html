Understanding Arizona's Continuing Education Requirements

As of 2026, Arizona requires real estate agents and brokers to complete 24 hours of continuing education every two years for license renewal. This includes mandatory courses in fair housing, agency law, contract law, disclosures, and real estate legal issues—each accounting for 3 hours. The remaining 9 hours are electives, offering flexibility for topics like visual marketing, which directly ties into real estate photography Arizona.

These requirements aren't just checkboxes; they're opportunities to deepen your expertise in professional real estate photos. For photographers, aligning with these CE trends means offering services that resonate with agents' needs, such as ethical drone usage or AI-enhanced editing. By staying updated, you ensure your work complies with state regulations while enhancing listing appeal.

Mandatory vs. Elective Courses: What to Prioritize

Focus on mandatory topics first to avoid compliance issues, but don't overlook electives in photography and marketing. Courses on virtual staging or 3D tours can provide the edge you need in competitive areas like Scottsdale or Chandler.
