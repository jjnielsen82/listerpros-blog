What drives this? Increased relocations and a preference for contactless experiences post-pandemic. In tech-forward areas, agents are quickly adopting 3D tours for Arizona real estate listings to meet buyer expectations.

This year, Arizona agents are blending Zillow 3D home tours with AI-enhanced video calls for hybrid viewings, seeing a 30% adoption spike in the last six months. This caters to buyers who want a guided, remote walkthrough before committing to travel.

Economic Drivers in Hot Markets

With Arizona's 2.5% annual population growth in 2026, 3D tours are key for standing out in places like Scottsdale, correlating with 10% higher offer rates from international buyers. In drought-prone regions, highlighting water-efficient features via tours differentiates listings.
