How to Add Zillow 3D Tours to Arizona Real Estate Listings

June 22, 2026
2 min read

Arizona real estate agents face intense competition in Phoenix, Scottsdale, and Tucson markets. Adding Zillow 3D tours to your listings gives buyers an immersive way to explore properties before scheduling showings.

Why Zillow 3D Tours Boost Listing Performance

Industry research shows that interactive 3D tours extend time on page and generate more qualified inquiries than photo galleries alone. In Arizona’s fast-paced markets, where many buyers relocate from out of state, these tours help showcase open floor plans, outdoor living spaces, and natural light that photos cannot fully convey.

Benefits for Phoenix and Tucson Listings

  • Higher click-through rates from remote buyers
  • Better highlighting of desert architecture and mountain views
  • Improved search visibility on major portals
  • Mobile-friendly self-guided walkthroughs

Step-by-Step Guide to Adding Zillow 3D Tours

Follow these practical steps to integrate tours smoothly into your workflow.

1. Schedule a Professional Scan Early

Book a 3D capture before professional photos are taken. This allows the model to be used across multiple platforms. Partner with a local provider experienced in real estate photography Arizona to ensure consistent lighting and accurate spatial representation.

2. Upload Directly Through Zillow

Log into your Premier Agent dashboard, open the listing editor, and select the 3D Tour upload option. Link tours created with approved tools such as Matterport. Confirm the tour meets Zillow’s technical requirements for smooth playback.

3. Optimize the Tour Experience

Add clear room labels and highlight key features like kitchens or backyards. Set the starting viewpoint to the most appealing angle. Test the tour on both iOS and Android devices to verify loading speed and navigation.

Combining 3D Tours with Professional Photography

3D tours work best alongside high-quality images. Professional HDR photography captures the visual impact that drives initial clicks, while 3D tours provide depth. Arizona agents who pair both elements see stronger engagement across Phoenix and Tucson listings.

Recommended Workflow

  1. Order photography and 3D scanning together for coordinated scheduling
  2. Use the same-day delivery option to meet tight listing deadlines
  3. Layer virtual staging onto the 3D model for vacant properties
  4. Embed the tour link in MLS remarks and email campaigns

Arizona Market Considerations

Phoenix and Tucson listings often feature pools, energy-efficient upgrades, and unique outdoor amenities. 3D tours excel at demonstrating these elements to seasonal migrants who rely on virtual previews. Local knowledge of community layouts and architectural styles helps create tours that resonate with Arizona buyers.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Uploading poorly lit or incomplete scans
  • Forgetting to update tour status after a sale
  • Neglecting mobile optimization
  • Relying on 3D tours without supporting photography

Measuring Results and Refining Your Approach

Review Zillow analytics to see which areas of the home receive the most attention. Adjust future scans based on these insights. Continue promoting tours across social channels and email to maximize reach in competitive Arizona markets.
