Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using Listing Photography

Even with the best intentions, pitfalls can extend your DOM. Here's what to watch out for in property photography Phoenix and Tucson.

  1. Skipping Pro Services: Using smartphone photos often leads to poor quality, adding 20-30 days to DOM. Invest in specialists who understand Arizona's bright lighting.
  2. Insufficient Variety: Post at least 20 images covering all key areas; neglecting backyards or kitchens deters buyers in visually driven markets.
  3. Neglecting Edits: Unedited photos with clutter or bad colors can turn off prospects. Professional post-production ensures polished results.
  4. Ignoring Feedback: If a listing lingers, refresh photos after 30 days to signal freshness and maintain momentum.
  5. Misleading Edits: Avoid photoshopping major flaws to prevent buyer distrust; accuracy builds credibility and speeds sales.
