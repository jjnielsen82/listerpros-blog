Common Mistakes to Avoid in Video Marketing

Steer clear of pitfalls that undermine your efforts.

  1. Poor Production Quality: Avoid shaky footage; invest in stabilization or pros to keep viewers engaged.
  2. Overloading Information: Keep videos under 2 minutes, focusing on 3-5 highlights.
  3. Ignoring Mobile Optimization: Format for vertical viewing, as 70% of searches are mobile.
  4. Neglecting CTAs and Analytics: Always include next steps and track performance.
  5. Generic Content: Localize with Arizona elements like desert sunsets for relevance.
