The Roots of Commercial Real Estate Photography

Commercial real estate photography wasn't always the high-tech powerhouse it is today. As digital cameras emerged in the late 1990s and early 2000s, the game changed. High-resolution images became standard, allowing for better detail and easier sharing online. In Arizona, where vast desert landscapes and urban sprawl define the market, this shift meant photographers could highlight unique features like expansive industrial parks near Phoenix Sky Harbor or historic commercial districts in Tucson.
