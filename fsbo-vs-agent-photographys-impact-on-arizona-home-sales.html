Avoiding Common Photography Mistakes

Steer clear of pitfalls that hinder sales:

  1. Smartphone-Only Photos: They often distort perspectives and fail to capture Arizona's unique lighting—opt for professional equipment instead.
  2. Skipping Staging: Cluttered spaces make homes look smaller; always declutter and arrange furniture for flow.
  3. Ignoring Timing: Shoot during cooler mornings to avoid harsh shadows on outdoor amenities.
  4. Neglecting Optimization: Unoptimized images reduce visibility; add metadata for better search rankings.
  5. Interior Focus Only: Balance with exteriors to highlight Arizona's scenic appeal.
