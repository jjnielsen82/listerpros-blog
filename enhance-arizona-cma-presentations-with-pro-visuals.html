Common Mistakes to Avoid in CMA Visuals

Even seasoned agents can falter with visuals. One frequent error is overloading presentations with low-quality images, which undermines credibility. Instead, opt for professional real estate photos to ensure clarity and impact.

  1. Ignoring mobile compatibility: Always preview on devices to avoid frustration in our mobile-first 2026 world.
  2. Neglecting data accuracy: Use current Arizona market data in charts to maintain trust—outdated visuals can lead to legal issues.
  3. Forgetting storytelling flow: Link each visual to a narrative point, like explaining how a graph shows competitive pricing for your Phoenix home.
  4. Overcomplicating designs: Stick to simple, high-contrast visuals rather than busy animations that distract from the analysis.
  5. Overloading with quantity: Follow NAR guidelines—limit to three data points per slide to prevent cognitive overload.

By sidestepping these pitfalls, you'll create CMAs that are professional and effective, especially in Arizona's competitive landscape.
